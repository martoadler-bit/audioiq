# Audio content

265 six-second loops, all real recordings (not synthesized), trimmed from full
tracks on Wikimedia Commons with ffmpeg and converted to 44.1kHz/16-bit stereo WAV.
Each exercise picks a random loop from `ProcessingAudioEngine.realLoopResources` on
every round, so the practice material keeps varying across a wide range of genres:
classical, military band, jazz, blues, folk, flamenco, gamelan, reggae, gospel,
world music, opera, chant, and more.

## No attribution required (186 loops)

CC0 or public domain (mostly US government works — military band recordings, plus
some CC0 original recordings). No restrictions, safe for commercial use with zero
obligations.
